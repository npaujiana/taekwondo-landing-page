The Journey to Black Belt

In traditional martial arts, each belt represents the life cycle of growth, perseverance, and maturity:

  1. White Belt The blank slate; innocence and willingness to learn.
  2. Yellow Belt The earth; the seed of technique is firmly planted.
  3. Green Belt The sprout breaking through the soil; rapid skill growth.
  4. Blue Belt The sky; reaching upwards as poomsae and kicks mature.
  5. Red Belt The fire & sun; immense power tempered by self-control.
  6. Black Belt (Dan) Mastery, humility, and the true beginning of wisdom.
